### Hi, I'm Kartikey.

I'm a software engineer. I have built software for more than ten years, for startups and small companies, many of them in the US. Most of it in Ruby on Rails.

Some of that work:

- **Sidecar Learning**, an interactive learning platform used by universities, including the University of Arizona and the University of Toronto. I joined as lead backend developer in 2018 and was CTO from 2020 to 2024. As CTO I cut its cloud costs in half and led its AI features.
- **Reword**, an AI startup for writers. As lead Rails engineer I worked directly with the founder, and helped it become profitable within 18 months.
- **A high-end keyboard brand** founded by Ryan Norbauer. I led the build of its e-commerce platform, from scratch, in Rails.
- **UltraAuth**, biometric login without passwords. I wrote a Ruby gem that was included in GitLab CE and passed 500,000 downloads. The company was later acquired.
- **Hero Group**, where I have been building Hero Hero, an internal AI agent that works inside the team's Slack and Basecamp. I wrote about it in [Removing the CTO as the Bottleneck](/2026/06/15/removing-the-cto-as-the-bottleneck.html) and [Your CTO Got 10x Faster](/2026/06/15/your-cto-got-10x-faster.html).

I also made [eKard](https://ekard.app/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, digital business cards for small businesses and professionals.

### Websites, with Sweta

My wife Sweta and I make websites for local businesses, as [Sweta and Kartikey](https://proposals.kartikey.dev/). Sweta designs each site and writes its words. I build it, put it live, and keep it fast and secure.

We make each new website as a sample first, so the owner can see it on their own phone before deciding anything. There's nothing to pay to look. [How it works](/websites/), or [send us your website](https://proposals.kartikey.dev/start).
