### What we make

People look up a local business on their phones: are they open, what's on today, how do we get there? So we design each site for the phone first, and then for computers.

**Sweta** designs your site and writes its words. She or Kartikey will be on your call.

**Kartikey** builds your site, puts it live on your web address, and keeps it fast and secure. He has built software for more than ten years, for startups and small companies, many of them in the US. [More about Kartikey](/about/).

### What a slow or confusing website costs a shop

Nobody writes to say they gave up on your website. They just go to the next place.

- From one second to three, the chance a visitor leaves a phone page rises 32%.
- 76% of people who search nearby on their phone visit a related business within a day.
- 73% say they're unlikely to go back to a business listed as open online that turned out to be closed.
- When Google shows an AI summary, people click through to a website on 8% of visits, against 15% without one.

[The research, and what we do about each](https://proposals.kartikey.dev/losing-customers)

### How our samples work

A website is hard to imagine and easy to judge, so we make a working sample first.

- You open it and click around. In a minute you know whether you'd like it.
- There's nothing to pay to look.
- Your own website isn't touched. Nothing changes on it until you say so.
- The sample isn't on Google, and we won't show it to anyone else. We'll delete it whenever you ask.

If we made one for you, it's at the link we sent you. If it's not for you, [ask us to take it down](mailto:hi@kartikey.dev?subject=Please%20take%20down%20my%20sample). We will, and we won't use your photos or anything from your site again.

### If you say yes

- You pay once, by card through Stripe. If for any reason we can't put it live, you get a full refund.
- We switch your web address over with you or whoever looks after it, on a short call or by email.
- Your new site is live within 48 hours of your yes: it's already built.
- Once you've paid, it's yours: the design, the words and the pictures, with the files whenever you ask.
